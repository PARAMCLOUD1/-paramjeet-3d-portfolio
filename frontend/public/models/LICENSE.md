`businesswoman.glb` is a project derivative: textures resized to at most 1024px,
opaque images converted to JPEG, transparent images palette-optimized; a 14-joint
humanoid skeleton, approximate spatial skin weights, and original `Walk` / `Idle`
keyframes added. Those project-authored asset changes are also dedicated under CC0.
This model represents a generic placeholder, not Paramjeet's face or body.
The source includes a silver pixie hairstyle and a tailored jacket and trousers.
No personal reference photos were uploaded or used to alter this model.

Rebuild: from `frontend`, run `node scripts/build-character.mjs`.
Verify geometry and clips: `node scripts/verify-character.mjs`.
The preserved original is `assets/character/businesswoman-source.glb` outside the
public directory; only the optimized derivative is delivered by the frontend.
The rig is an approximate placeholder rig; fine joint deformation and likeness
need a human artist's review before presenting it as a final personalized character.
